FRACTURED V4 — Single Goblin Prototype (V32)

Upload these files to the ROOT of your Fractured-V4 repository:
  main.js
  index.html
  goblinEnemy.js
  goblin/ (entire folder, including every PNG)

Replace only main.js and index.html; goblinEnemy.js and goblin/ are new.
Do not remove or overwrite player.js, angelKnightSpriteRenderer.js,
bridge/world files, the panorama, or existing knight PNGs.

The enemy appears just to the right of the player when entering the world.
Its 100-HP bar is above its head. It patrols, approaches, attacks, blocks,
jumps, lands, and plays hurt/death frames. The knight's attack hitboxes
can damage it. There is exactly one goblin; it does not respawn.

Source: supplied goblin animation sheet. Frame cutouts are prototype
extractions from the generated sheet; art cleanup can be done later.
