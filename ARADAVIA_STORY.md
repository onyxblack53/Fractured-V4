# Aradavia — confirmed story foundations

## Nation and playable characters
- Aradavia is a prosperous nation of advanced magic and technology.
- A council of thirteen scholars and powerful arcane wielders governs it.
- Playable choices: Angelic Knight or Demonic Rogue. No mixed bloodlines or separate class selection.
- Opening format: cinematic video if possible, otherwise alternating animated still images.

## The Black Gem
1. The council discovers a power buried deep beneath the earth: the Black Gem.
2. All thirteen swear to seal it away.
3. One scholar seeks to use its power to advance Aradavia further into its golden age.
4. During the ritual to seal the gem in the void, the thirteenth scholar tethers the darkness to himself.
5. The gem is sealed, but the darkness has already found its new host.
6. Influenced by the darkness, the scholar descends into madness, killing fellow scholars and draining their power for himself.
7. The darkness reveals a ritual: a dark, forgotten curse powerful enough to send the world into an age of darkness.

8. As the scholar performs the ritual, the earth and sky crack open.
9. Other worlds spill through the fractures, unleashing nightmarish creatures of hell.

## Transition into gameplay
Immediately after the Fracture and the creatures of hell are unleashed, the cinematic ends and the player chooses Angelic Knight or Demonic Rogue. No additional story scene precedes this choice.

The opening currently ends with the Fracture and the unleashing of creatures of hell. Existing landscape images are temporary cinematic backdrops, not literal depictions of the council, gem, or golden age.
